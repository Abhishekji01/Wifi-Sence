    How it decides

    Presence
    Signal energy in 0.1 to 0.6 Hz rises well above the empty-room baseline
    Breathing
    One clear peak between 0.2 and 0.5 Hz (12 to 30 per minute), rate = 60 × peak frequency
    Fall
    A sharp burst of movement, then stillness for several seconds while someone is still present
    Window
    20 second sliding window, updated twice a second
    Thresholds
    Set from your own empty-room calibration, not hard-coded
